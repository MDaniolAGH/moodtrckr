# Recommendation

The client should be able to decide after reading one page.

## Principles

- **Answer first.** The first sentence answers the decision statement.
- **Justify with this case.** Every reason points to the brief, client answers, observations or
  the options comparison — not to general best practice.
- **Honest scope.** Say what the recommendation does not solve and what the client must still do.
- **Falsifiable.** State what new information would change the recommendation.

## Structure

1. **Recommendation** — one sentence answering the decision.
2. **Why** — 3–5 reasons, each with its evidence.
3. **Scope** — what is included, what is explicitly not.
4. **Cost and responsibilities** — one-off, monthly, and what stays with the client.
5. **Risks and how they are handled** — top 3.
6. **What would change this** — the assumptions it depends on.
7. **Next step** — one concrete action and who takes it.

Write the output in the language of the client brief; keep it to one page.

## Done when

- A reader who saw only this page could explain the choice and its cost to a colleague.
- Nothing in it relies on assumptions from other projects.
